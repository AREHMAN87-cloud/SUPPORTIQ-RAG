Warranty Policy

Electronics and appliances sold on our platform carry the manufacturer's standard warranty, typically ranging from 6 months to 1 year depending on the brand and product category. Warranty covers manufacturing defects only and does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or misuse.

To claim warranty service, customers should keep their original invoice and contact the manufacturer's authorized service center directly, or reach out to our support team for guidance on the nearest service center.

Non-electronic items (clothing, accessories, home goods) do not carry a warranty but are covered under the standard 14-day return policy for manufacturing defects.
