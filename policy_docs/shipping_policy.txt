Shipping Policy

We ship across Pakistan using TCS and Leopards Courier. Standard delivery within major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi) takes 2-4 business days. Delivery to other cities may take 4-7 business days.

Shipping is free on orders above PKR 3,000. Orders below this threshold have a flat shipping fee of PKR 150.

Once an order is dispatched, customers receive a tracking number via SMS and email. Orders cannot be redirected to a different address after dispatch.

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available nationwide. Prepaid orders via JazzCash, Easypaisa, or bank transfer are processed faster and dispatched within 24 hours.
