General FAQ

Order tracking: You can track your order status from the "My Orders" section of your account, or via the tracking link sent by SMS/email after dispatch.

Payment methods: We accept Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer, and major debit/credit cards.

Order cancellation: Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are dispatched. Once dispatched, cancellation is not possible and the order must be returned after delivery following the standard return policy.

Account issues: For login or account access issues, use the "Forgot Password" option or contact support with your registered phone number.

Bulk orders: For bulk or wholesale orders, please contact our business support team directly for custom pricing and delivery timelines.
