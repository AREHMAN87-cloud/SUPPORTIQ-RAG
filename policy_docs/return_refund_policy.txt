Return and Refund Policy

Customers may return most products within 14 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging with all tags attached.

Items excluded from returns: innerwear, cosmetics, perishable goods, and customized/personalized products.

To initiate a return, customers must submit a return request through their order history or contact support with the order number. Once the returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. For COD orders, refunds are issued via bank transfer or store credit.

Exchanges for a different size or color are processed the same way as returns, subject to stock availability.
